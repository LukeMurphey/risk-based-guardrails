Methodology: This calculator runs 10,000 Monte Carlo simulations over your selected retirement horizon. Stock returns use S&P 500 historical parameters: nominal mean 10.3%, standard deviation 17.1% (1970–2024). Bond returns use 10-year US Treasury parameters: nominal mean 5.2%, standard deviation 7.8%. Cash earns a flat 4.5%. Inflation is modeled at 3.0% annually. Returns are drawn from a log-normal distribution each year. Fees are deducted from the relevant asset class return. The simulation finds the maximum annual withdrawal (inflation-adjusted) such that at least 80% of runs end with a positive portfolio balance. The Kitces guardrails framework uses a 95% upper guardrail (increase spending) and 70% lower guardrail (decrease spending) based on the probability of success of your current withdrawal amount. Read the original methodology at Kitces.com.
